Crynwr NE2000 packet driver path
================================

NE2KUP.BAT expects:

  C:\O300NET\CRYNWR\NE2000.COM

NE2KDN.BAT unloads packet interrupt 0x66 using TERMIN.COM from either:

  C:\O300NET\CRYNWR\TERMIN.COM
  C:\LXNET\TERMIN.COM

The intended Crynwr source is the fragglet mirror of Russ Nelson's DOS
packet drivers:

  https://github.com/fragglet/crynwr_mirror

Tested command shape:

  O300NIC 5
  CRYNWR\NE2000.COM 0x66 5 0x300
  TCPUP
  C:\MTCP\PING.EXE gateway-address
